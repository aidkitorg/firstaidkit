Gertrude is a 63-year-old grandmother from Decatur, Illinois who unexpectedly became the primary caregiver for her three grandchildren after her daughter's struggles with addiction. The children are 8, 12, and 16 years old, and adjusting to life with their grandmother has been challenging for everyone. Her fixed income from social security and a small pension totals around $28,000 annually, making every dollar count. Gertrude worked as a school secretary for 30 years before retiring, and those organizational skills now help her manage doctor appointments, school meetings, and extracurricular activities for three active kids. She's become an advocate in local support groups for grandparents raising grandchildren. Despite the unexpected turn her retirement took, Gertrude finds purpose in giving her grandchildren stability and unconditional love. The 16-year-old is showing promise academically, and Gertrude is determined to help him get to college.
