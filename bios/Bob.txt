Bob is 55 and lives in Springfield, Illinois, where he works as a senior project manager for a construction company. He and his wife raised three children—now aged 18, 21, and 25—who are in varying stages of college and early careers. With an income hovering around $150,000, Bob has been able to pay down his mortgage and start planning for retirement, though tuition bills keep him grounded. A lifelong Cubs fan, he often takes weekend trips to Chicago with his youngest son to catch games. He’s passionate about mentoring younger employees at work and volunteers with Habitat for Humanity on the weekends, passing on the trade skills he learned from his own father.
