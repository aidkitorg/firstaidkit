Alice grew up in the south suburbs of Chicago and has built her life around community and family. At 39, she balances working as a paralegal at a mid-sized law firm with raising her two kids, a 7-year-old boy and a 10-year-old girl. Her income of around $70,000 a year allows her to maintain a modest but comfortable home in Oak Lawn, though she’s constantly budgeting to cover extracurriculars like soccer leagues and piano lessons. Alice is deeply involved in her church and volunteers with a local food pantry twice a month. She dreams of going back to school for her law degree one day, though for now she finds fulfillment in being a steady presence for her kids and modeling resilience.
