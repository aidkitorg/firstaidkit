John is a 41-year-old restaurant owner in Springfield who runs a small family diner that's been struggling since the pandemic. He and his wife have five children ranging in age from 1 to 18, making their household constantly busy and loud. His income fluctuates dramatically - sometimes reaching $95,000 in good years, but dropping as low as $40,000 when business is slow. The oldest just started college on a partial scholarship, while the youngest is still in diapers. John works 70-hour weeks trying to keep the restaurant afloat while his wife manages the home front. He's known for hiring teenagers for their first jobs and teaching them work ethic. Despite the financial uncertainty, John loves being his own boss and providing a gathering place for the community. His specialty is a breakfast burrito that locals say is the best in central Illinois.
