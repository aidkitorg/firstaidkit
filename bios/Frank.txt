Frank is a 52-year-old high school history teacher in Joliet, Illinois who has dedicated his life to education. He and his wife have been married for 25 years and have three children: a 22-year-old recent college graduate struggling to find work, a 19-year-old sophomore studying engineering, and a 15-year-old who plays varsity tennis. His teacher's salary of $72,000 has allowed the family to maintain a middle-class lifestyle, though college expenses have forced them to take on some debt. Frank runs the school's debate team and has a reputation for making history come alive for his students. He spends summers doing home improvement projects and has slowly renovated most of their 1970s ranch house himself. Frank worries about his oldest child's job prospects but remains optimistic about the power of education to open doors.
