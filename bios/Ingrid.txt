Ingrid is a 44-year-old veterinary technician from Evanston, Illinois who has always had a passion for animals. She has one child, a 13-year-old son who inherited her love of nature and volunteers at the local animal shelter. Her income of $54,000 allows them to live modestly in a small apartment, though she dreams of buying a house with a yard where they could foster rescue dogs. Ingrid immigrated from Sweden fifteen years ago and still speaks with a slight accent that her patients find charming. She specializes in exotic animal care and often takes emergency calls for the local zoo. Her son is academically gifted and interested in marine biology, which makes Ingrid proud but also worried about college costs. She works extra shifts during holidays to save money and has started a small side business doing pet photography to supplement her income.
