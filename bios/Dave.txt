Dave is a 42-year-old factory worker from Aurora who has been with the same manufacturing plant for 15 years. He and his partner Maria are raising four children together - ages 3, 9, 14, and 17. His income of $58,000 provides a stable foundation for the family, though with four kids the budget is always tight. Dave is known for his dry sense of humor and his ability to fix almost anything mechanical. He coaches youth basketball at the community center and is passionate about teaching kids the value of teamwork. The oldest is considering trade school, which Dave enthusiastically supports, having learned that college isn't the only path to success. He dreams of buying a small farm where the family could grow their own vegetables and maybe raise a few chickens.
